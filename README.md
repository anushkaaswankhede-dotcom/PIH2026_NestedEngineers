# PIH2026_NestedEngineers
India-Focused CO₂ Emission Analysis and Intelligent Tree Plantation Mapping System

BACKGROUND OF THE IDEA:
India is facing a rapid increase in carbon dioxide (CO₂) emissions due to industrialization, urban expansion, transportation growth, and energy consumption. Rising emissions contribute to:
1.Climate change
2.Poor air quality
3.Increased temperature
4.Health issues
5.Agricultural productivity loss

At the same time, tree cover in many districts is declining, and plantation efforts are often not data-driven. 
Trees are planted without considering:
1.Local soil type
2.Climate suitability
3.Regional emission levels
4.Long-term carbon sequestration potential
As a result, many plantation drives fail to create measurable environmental impact.

CORE IDEA:
There is no integrated, map-based, India-focused digital platform that:
1.Visualizes CO₂ emissions region-wise
2.Shows tree coverage data district-wise
3.Estimates the number of trees required to offset emissions
4.Recommends suitable tree species based on soil type and environmental conditions
5.Enables individuals and industries to calculate their carbon footprint and offset requirements
This lack of data-driven decision-making reduces the effectiveness of environmental restoration efforts.

Problem Statement:

How can we design an interactive, India-specific environmental web platform that:

1. Visualizes carbon emissions and tree coverage using GIS-based maps
2.Calculates the number of trees required to offset personal and industrial CO₂ emissions
4.Recommends region-appropriate tree species based on soil type (clay, sandy, loamy) and climatic conditions
5.Displays environmental analytics, predictions, and real-time air quality indicators
6.Encourages community participation through plantation tracking and gamification

Objective:

To develop a map-based environmental intelligence system that enables:

- Data-driven plantation planning
- Region-specific carbon offset strategies
- Improved awareness of carbon footprint
- Sustainable ecological restoration in India

Expected Impact

1.Optimized tree plantation efforts
2.Increased carbon sequestration efficiency
3.Reduction in regional carbon imbalance
4.Enhanced environmental awareness
5.Support for climate action and ESG initiatives
